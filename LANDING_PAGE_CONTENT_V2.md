## How It Works — From your content to intelligent answers.

Three steps to launch, five deterministic stages under the hood.

### Get live in 3 steps
1. **Design your agent** — name it, set its personality and brand voice, choose its channels. No code.
2. **Add your knowledge** — upload documents or point Lumjha at your website. We do the rest.
3. **Publish & connect** — embed the widget or connect WhatsApp, Instagram, Facebook, and Telegram. You're live.

### The knowledge pipeline (under the hood)
1. **Ingestion** — files uploaded or website crawled; metadata stored securely.
2. **Text extraction** — content lifted from PDF, Word, Excel, Markdown, and plain text into clean, readable text.
3. **Chunking** — large content split into logical sections, each tagged with topic, section, and page.
4. **Embedding** — each chunk converted to a vector that captures meaning, not just keywords.
5. **Storage & retrieval (RAG)** — raw files to secure storage, vectors to the vector database. At query time: analyze the question → semantic search for the most relevant chunks → blend with conversation history → generate a grounded, contextual answer.

_Supported file types: PDF · DOCX · XLSX · Markdown · TXT_

## FAQ

**Do I need to know how to code?**
No. Lumjha is fully no-code — design your agent, add your content, and publish.

**How does the agent know about my business?**
It learns exclusively from the documents you upload and the website you connect, using retrieval-augmented generation (RAG). Answers are grounded in your data, not the open internet.

**Which channels are supported?**
Website widget, WhatsApp, Instagram, Facebook, and Telegram — plus Shopify and Google Calendar integrations. The number of simultaneous channels depends on your plan.

**What happens after the 14-day trial?**
Your Chatbot, Site Search, and Policy Vault are free during the trial. After it ends, the Chatbot stays active on any paid plan; Site Search and Policy Vault can be added on. Your data is always preserved — never deleted.

**What's the difference between Site Search and Policy Vault?**
Site Search returns fast, summarized answers across all your content. Policy Vault is built for high-stakes documents — it verifies every quote verbatim and cites the exact source.

**Is my data secure?**
Yes. Your knowledge base is isolated to your workspace, protected by authenticated access and secure sessions, and never used to train shared models or exposed to other clients.

**Can I cancel anytime?**
Yes — no contracts, no commitments. Your data stays preserved even if you downgrade.
